1. Prefer editorial / partnership links over “SEO directories.”
2. One NAP identity: **Nijverheidsstraat 63, 7575 BH Oldenzaal**, phone **+31 541 534 982**, site **https://www.mccoy.nl**.

4. Do not invent awards, client logos, or press quotes for outreach.

- Automated “500 directories” packages
- Guest-post marketplaces selling links
- Exact-match anchor stuffing (“beste schoonmaakbedrijf Twente”)
- Reciprocal link schemes with unrelated sites
- Forum / comment spam
